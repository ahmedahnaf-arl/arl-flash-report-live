SYSTEMIC PATTERN: Four SBUs are blocked by the same unresolved LC approval problem — Bongo Coal (3 LCs), Nobayon (import 18.63 Cr since January), AEFL (no product), and ACL F&B (September LCs). This is the single largest controllable revenue leak across the group. The procurement approval model raised false flags in the prior meeting and remains unrectified. If LC approvals were unblocked today, Bongo Coal + Nobayon import + Electrofab combined represent ~40+ Cr of near-term recoverable revenue.
