Key Issues

Trading / Import Shortage

AEL Trading has no product or import. NTL's 55 Cr import did not materialise, leaving ~70 Cr of product unavailable (forecast ~34.7 Cr). BTL's 50,000 MT order will only be delivered in January.

Bitumen LC Risk

Bitumen has no stock. A third-party import arrangement is proposed to minimise LC risk; sourcing rocks in-house is also under review.

Product Availability — AEL

NPD launch deferred due to capacity and storage constraints; sourcing challenged by price hikes; some products held back for future profitability. Imported lentil and chickpea prices remain elevated, so sales are on hold.

Profitability & Ramp-up

Several SBUs — ACL F&B, AEFL, LifeCare and NextJobz — are operating below profitable scale. The focus is on clearing the underlying blockers (stock, LC, workforce, outlet ramp-up) rather than on the gap itself.

Workforce & Outlet Gaps

AMPL's sales team has resigned, disrupting sales. AEFL has no workforce mobilised yet. LifeCare runs 35 of 43 budgeted outlets, each needing ~18 months to mature.

AITL Stock Utilisation

AITL holds 12 Cr of stock. It is proposed that when procuring gift items for other SBUs' campaigns, this inventory can be explored — not that the entire stock will be distributed.
