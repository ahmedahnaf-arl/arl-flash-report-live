Key Issues

Trading Shortage (~353 Cr)

AEL Trading, BTL and NTL account for the largest gap. No stock, no import for four months, and NTL's product is stuck at port. AEL Trading needs MD intervention; the internal system is a key blocker.

Ledger Mismatch (ALL & AAL)

Ledger mismatch in the system — no ledger or sales-order visibility, hampering invoice generation. Long payment cycle and additional funding needed to grow volume.

Product Availability — AEL

Consumer and bulk channels both lagging (~7 Cr gap each). Dal portfolio missing SKUs (yellow peas, chickpeas), NPD shortfall, rice portfolio gaps, and salt supply held up by a supply-side legal dispute.

Fund Constraint — ACEL

Only 5 of 15 budgeted outlets open. Sourcing and outlet funding limited; a 10 Cr loan from Cement released only 1.5 Cr. MD reviewing the SBU.

Leadership Transition — BPL & AITL

Both granted a 3-month transition under new leadership. New budgets to be issued for January; ~2.5 Cr/month expected from BPL in the interim.
